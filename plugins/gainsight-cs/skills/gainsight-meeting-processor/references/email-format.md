# Email Format Reference (CSM)

Email tone and structure for the general Gainsight CSM motion. Writing rules: no em dashes, no emojis, no filler openers, plain prose.

## Subject Line

```
[Customer] & Gainsight – [MM/DD/YYYY] – [Call Type] recap
```

Examples:
- <Account> & Gainsight – 05/20/2026 – QBR recap
- <Account> & Gainsight – 05/20/2026 – Renewal Planning recap
- <Account> & Gainsight – 05/20/2026 – Product Issue Discussion recap

## Tone & Style

- Personal and direct. Like a knowledgeable colleague writing to people they've been working with.
- Opening is genuine and specific to what actually happened on the call. Never generic.
- Action items addressed directly to each person by name. Not a generic table.
- Resources explained briefly: what they are for, who should use them.
- Length scales with call complexity: ad-hoc syncs are short, QBRs and at-risk calls are longer.
- **No em dashes.** Use periods, colons, commas, or restructure.
- **No emojis.** No hollow openers like "Thank you for your valuable time".

## Universal Structure

### 1. Greeting

Small / familiar groups (1-3 people):
```
Good [morning/afternoon], [First Name] and [First Name],
```

Larger or earlier-stage groups:
```
Good [morning/afternoon], [Customer] team,
```

### 2. Opening (1-2 sentences)

Specific to this call. Reference what was discussed or notable.

GOOD: "Thanks for working through the integration issue with us today, and for the detail on the high-touch account scenarios."
GOOD: "It was good to align on the phased rollout plan and the cleanup approach we discussed."
BAD: "Thank you for your time today."

### 3. Recording block (always)

```
Here is the information to access today's recording:
[INSERT RECORDING LINK]
Passcode: [INSERT PASSCODE]
```

### 4. Resources (when applicable)

Used when links, guides, or docs were shared. Brief note on each:

```
Resources from today's call:
- <Resource name> – for the team to reference when reviewing <topic>
- <Resource name> – worth circulating to the broader CSM team
```

For customer-specific resources, call out the relevant person:
```
<First name> – I've shared the <resource> draft with you. We'll send the populated version by EOW.
```

### 5. Action items by person

Each owner gets a named paragraph. Direct address.

For numbered items to one person:
```
<First name> – A couple of items for you:
1. <Specific action with enough detail to be actionable>.
2. <Specific action with enough detail to be actionable>.
```

Single item to one person:
```
<First name> – Please <specific action with the team / context>.
```

For the CSM's own actions:
```
On our end, we will <specific action> and share the result next week. We will also <follow-up action>.
```

### 6. Scheduling / next steps

Always attributed. Times in customer's stated TZ or EST default.

```
We are scheduled to reconnect on <date> to review <topic>. I will send a meeting invite shortly.
```

When availability is needed:
```
<First name> – Please find my availability below, times in EST. Let me know what works best.
[Day, Date]: [time range] OR [time range]
```

### 7. Closing (one line)

```
Please reach out if anything comes up before our next sync.
```
```
I appreciate the time and the partnership working through this.
```

### 8. No sign-off

The user's Gmail signature template handles name / title / email. Do not include a sign-off in the generated body.

## Call-Type Guidance

### Kickoff
Tone: Energetic, welcoming. Excitement about starting together.

### QBR / EBR
Tone: Strategic, executive-friendly. Reference business outcomes, ROI, roadmap alignment.
Required: KPI / metric callouts, success-plan progress, forward-looking initiatives.

### At-Risk Call
Tone: Direct, accountable, no platitudes. Acknowledge the issue head-on.
Required: explicit recovery actions with owners + due dates, executive escalation path if relevant, next-touch commitment.

### Renewal Conversation
Tone: Partnership-oriented. Ground in value already delivered.
Required: value-realization summary, commercial next steps, decision-maker alignment.

### Cadence / Ad-hoc Sync
Tone: Efficient, no ceremony.
Required: recording link, short action items list, next-touch.

### Closeout / Handover
Tone: Warm, celebratory if positive ending; clean, grateful if neutral.
Required: NPS survey reference (if applicable), handover contact intro, final action items.

---

## Assembling the Email

Include only the sections relevant to the call:

1. Greeting
2. Opening
3. Recording block
4. Resources (if applicable)
5. Action items by person
6. Scheduling / next call
7. Closing line — stop here, no sign-off

Reads as if the CSM wrote it — personal, direct, specific to this customer and call. Never templated-feeling.
