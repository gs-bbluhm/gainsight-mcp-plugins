# Staircase for account teams

For CSMs and account managers. With this pack, Claude reads your customer conversations through the Staircase AI connector and tells you what needs you today, which renewals need work, and which accounts have gone quiet, then hands you each next step ready to go: the customer email drafted, the Slack ask to support, product or your account executive written, the meeting or hold proposed. Ask in plain words in Claude, the way you would ask a teammate; nobody on your team has to learn the Staircase app. The pack works on its own once the connector is connected, and nothing is sent without your yes.

Skills: `staircase-csm-today` (what needs you today, or this week) and `staircase-renewals` (which renewals need work, with the play and the draft).
