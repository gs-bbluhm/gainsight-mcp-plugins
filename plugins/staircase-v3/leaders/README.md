# Staircase for leaders

For CS managers. With this pack, Claude reads your team's customer conversations through the Staircase AI connector and shows how your team's accounts are doing: load and exposure per person (alphabetical, never ranked), up to ten accounts worth leaning into, and what's renewing on the team, with a Slack note to the account owner ready before any email to a customer sponsor. Ask in plain words in Claude; nobody has to learn the Staircase app. The pack works on its own once the connector is connected, and nothing is sent without your yes.

Skills: `staircase-manager-team`.

## Install (beta)
